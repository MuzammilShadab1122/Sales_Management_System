1. How Mobile Viewing Works When Laptop is OFF

Whenever you enter any sale or payment on the laptop, this software automatically updates all Excel files AND a standalone mobile app file (Rajpoot_Traders_Mobile_Viewer.html) inside Excel_Sheets/.

1
Install Free Google Drive for Desktop on this laptop and sign in with the same Google account used on the client's Android/iPhone.
2
Set the Cloud Mirror Folder below (or add D:\My Projects\Sales Management System\Excel_Sheets to Google Drive sync).
3
Even when the laptop is shut down at the shop, the client can open the Google Drive / Google Sheets app on their mobile in any city, view every shopkeeper's khata & stock, and forward bills on WhatsApp with Rs. 0 monthly expense!
